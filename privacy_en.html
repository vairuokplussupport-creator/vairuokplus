1. Who we are

Vairuok+ is an independently developed driver-assistance app. It is not a Bolt product, partner, official integration or Bolt-approved app. Third-party transport platform names are used solely to describe compatibility. The publisher and data controller are identified in this policy.

2. Information processed

Trip offers. After an affirmative choice and Android Accessibility permission, the service examines text elements in the active window of a supported driver app (currently Bolt Driver), selecting the offer price, distance to pickup, trip distance and duration. Other text, including place names or addresses, may occur in the same window. The app does not automatically accept trips.

Optional OCR and screen capture. If the user enables screen capture, Android requests separate MediaProjection permission. Depending on the capture scope selected, images may contain content from other apps, addresses or other personal information. Images are processed on the device for text recognition. Live OCR diagnostics store counts of recognized price, distance and time formats and processing duration, not a history of screenshots or complete recognized text. A photo manually selected by the user is also processed on device. Screen capture is optional and can be stopped.

Local settings. Language, vehicle, fuel type, consumption, fuel prices, profitability thresholds, V+ position and permission/consent states may be saved on the device.

Diagnostics. Local records may contain service states, event timestamps, scan counts, durations, pattern detection indicators and errors.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Optional offer analysis relies on the user’s consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a), where personal data are processed). Local preferences support requested functionality (Article 6(1)(b), where applicable). Voluntary support requests are processed on the basis of legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) – responding to users, troubleshooting technical issues and improving app functionality, insofar as those interests are not overridden by users’ rights and freedoms. Estimates do not constitute legally significant automated decision-making. If Accessibility consent is not given, automatic offer analysis does not operate. Agreeing to Accessibility access does not itself authorize screen capture; Android requests separate confirmation for MediaProjection.

4. Transfers and Google ML Kit

In brief. Vairuok+ does not require a user account and does not store trip offers, screenshots, or OCR results on the developer’s servers. Trip analysis takes place on the user’s device. Vehicle, fuel, and language settings and technical diagnostics are stored on the device. The developer directly receives contact details and support-message information provided by the user only if the user voluntarily sends a support email. Google ML Kit may separately process certain technical data, as explained below.

Offer text, screen images and OCR recognition results are processed on the device and are not automatically uploaded to a Vairuok+-operated server. Google ML Kit processes OCR input and results on device, but may send Google technical telemetry, including device and app information, per-installation identifiers, processing times, event and error codes, and other performance metrics. Google states that this telemetry is transmitted over HTTPS. See Google ML Kit data disclosure. If a user voluntarily emails support, the chosen email service also processes the message.

5. Bug reports

Selecting “Report a bug” opens an email app with a prepared message and our support email address as the recipient. The message may include the description entered by the user, the issue type and technical details such as the app and Android versions and device model. Email is sent only if the user confirms sending; no screenshot is attached automatically. Please avoid including unnecessary personal data. If you email us, we receive your email address, the message and any attachments you choose to include. We use Google Gmail to receive and store support messages. The publisher reviews messages only to respond to support requests.

6. Recipients, security and international processing

Local settings and diagnostics are stored in Android app-private storage under operating-system access controls. We do not operate our own server for retaining offer text or screenshots. Support emails and attachments sent by users are received via Google Gmail; Google also receives some ML Kit technical telemetry. The selected sending app may process messages under its own terms. Google services may process data outside the European Economic Area; see Google Privacy Policy for the provider’s transfer and protection arrangements. Contact the controller at the address provided for information about the safeguards relevant to a particular transfer. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive details in support messages.

7. Retention, deletion and backups

Local settings and diagnostic information remain on the device until the user clears app data in Android settings or uninstalls the app. Android automatic backups are disabled in the app configuration, with additional exclusions for data transfers; device-manufacturer behavior may vary. Support emails and attachments received by Vairuok+ are normally deleted within 90 days of receipt, except where longer retention is necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to address a specific dispute. Third-party email providers may apply separate retention periods.

8. Permissions, choices and your rights

You can withdraw screen-reading consent in app settings, which turns off automatic offer analysis. Android permissions can also be disabled in system settings. Local data can be cleared in Android app storage settings. Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability and withdraw consent. Contact: vairuokplus.support@gmail.com. You may complain to the Lithuanian Data Protection Inspectorate: https://vdai.lrv.lt/. Without Accessibility permission, automatic analysis is unavailable; without display-over-other-apps permission, the V+ overlay cannot appear; without screen-capture permission, optional screen OCR cannot run. Sending a support email is voluntary.

9. Changes

If processing or features change, this policy will be updated and fresh consent sought when required.
